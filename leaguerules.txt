
🥥 Survivor Fantasy League Rules
1. The Draft & Roster
Before episode one, we will snake-draft our castaways. Each manager will draft a locked roster of 3–4 players. You cannot drop or trade players. When your castaway does something on the show, your team earns points.

2. The Commissioner
I will act as the league commissioner, tracking scores in a shared spreadsheet updated every Thursday morning. For subjective categories (like "first person to cry"), the commissioner’s ruling is final.

3. The Stakes
[Optional: Insert entry fee here, e.g., $10 buy-in for a winner-takes-all pot, or the loser has to eat a gross "Survivor-style" bug at the finale watch party!]

4. Scoring System
Points are cumulative. If your castaway wins an individual immunity (+5) and survives the weekly tribal council (+2), they earn 7 points for your team that week.

Survival Points

+2 Surviving the weekly Tribal Council

+5 Making the Merge

+10 Making the Final Three

+20 Winning Sole Survivor

Challenge Points

+5 Winning Individual Immunity

+3 Winning Individual Reward

+2 Winning a Tribal/Team Challenge

-1 Sitting out of a challenge

Advantage Points

+4 Finding a Hidden Immunity Idol or Advantage

+5 Successfully playing an Idol (negates votes)

+5 Correctly playing a Shot in the Dark

-5 Getting voted out with an Idol in their pocket

Social & Drama Points

+2 First person to cry in an episode

+1 Getting bleeped for swearing

-3 Medical Evacuation

-10 Quitting the game
